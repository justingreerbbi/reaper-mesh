Reaper Mesh Mobile app

Off-grid comms for your team

Reaper Mesh pairs your phone with a Reaper Mesh radio over Bluetooth. Send messages, share positions and see your team on the map over LoRa, with no cell signal and no internet.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Comms

Channels and direct messages with real delivery states, and quick brevity phrases you can send in two taps.

Map

Your team's positions, points and areas, distance measuring and an MGRS or decimal readout. Areas you've viewed stay available offline.

Nodes

Everyone your radio has heard, when, and how far away, with discovery on demand.

Device

Live status for your radio and every setting, without plugging in a computer.

Private by design

Channels can be encrypted with keys shared by QR code, and team networks rotate their keys on a schedule. There are no accounts, no tracking and no servers of ours: your messages, contacts and keys stay on your phone in an encrypted store. Read the privacy policy.

Built for gloves and mounted phones

Large touch targets, a night palette that keeps your eyes adjusted, and a layout that works in portrait or landscape on a vest, dash or handlebar mount.

Use it today

The same app runs in your browser now. Flash a supported board over USB, then connect from the web portal in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
